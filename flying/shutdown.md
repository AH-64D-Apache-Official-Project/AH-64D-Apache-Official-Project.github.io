1. Land, collective fully down, and hold the aircraft still.
2. **Weapons**: de-action everything and set the armament panel to **SAFE**.
3. **NVS mode**: **OFF**.
4. **Power levers**: **IDLE**. Let the engines run at idle for a minute or two so TGT comes down. A hot engine restarted too soon starts hot.
5. **Power levers**: **OFF**. Ng and Nr run down.
6. **Rotor brake**: **BRK** once Nr is below 50%. The brake does nothing above that. It stops the rotor in well under a minute; set **LOCK** to hold it.
7. **APU**: off, if it is running.
8. **MSTR IGN**: **OFF**. The displays go dark.
9. **HDU**: stow the monocle.
{: .steps}

> The `ROTOR BRAKE ON`{: .mpd} advisory with both levers off is normal. The `RTR BRK ON/LK`{: .mpd} *caution* means a power lever is off its OFF stop with the brake set.
{: .note}
