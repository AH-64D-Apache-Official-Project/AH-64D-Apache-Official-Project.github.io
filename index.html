AH-64D Apache Longbow

A two-seat attack helicopter with a clickable cockpit, its own flight model, working MPD pages, IHADSS symbology, a fire control radar and laser and radar guided Hellfires. This manual covers all of it, from installing the mod to flying a Hellfire engagement.

Before you fly

The mod needs four other mods loaded with it.

CBA_A3Settings, keybinds, events ACE3Missile guidance and laser Hatchet Interaction FrameworkClickable cockpit HeliSim CoreFlight model and systems

Help and feedback

Ask on the Discord first; most problems are a missing mod or a setting. Confirmed bugs go to the issue tracker. This manual is open source: every page has an edit link at the bottom.
