AH-64D Apache Longbow

The AH-64D Block I: a two-seat attack helicopter with an operable cockpit, the HeliSim flight model, MPD pages, IHADSS symbology, fire control radar, and laser- and radar-guided Hellfires. This manual covers installation, the aircraft systems and their limits, normal and emergency procedures, the displays and sensors, and weapon engagement.
